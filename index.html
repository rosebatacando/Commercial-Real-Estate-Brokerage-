The Basics

What Does A CRE Broker Actually Do?

A licensed professional who represents buyers, sellers, landlords, or tenants in commercial property transactions.

They source deals, run comps, negotiate terms, and manage the paperwork that gets a transaction to the closing table.

Sheet 02 / 09
Know The Players

Listing Agent vs. Buyer's Rep

Sell-Side

Listing Agent

  • Represents the seller/landlord
  • Markets the property
  • Negotiates for their client's best price
  • Typically paid by the seller
Buy-Side

Buyer's / Tenant's Rep

  • Represents the buyer/tenant
  • Sources off-market & listed deals
  • Negotiates favorable terms for you
  • Often paid via commission split

Follow The Commission

How Brokers Get Paid

On a sale, commission is typically 3–6% of the purchase price — usually split between the listing broker and the buyer's broker.

Anatomy Of A CRE Deal

Letter of Intent (LOI)Non-binding terms are proposed to open negotiations.
Due DiligenceFinancials, inspections, and title are verified.
Purchase & Sale AgreementThe binding contract is signed by both parties.
ClosingFinancing finalizes, funds transfer, keys change hands.

Vet Your Broker

Ask These Before You Sign

•
Who do you represent?You exclusively, or is this dual agency?
•
What's your track record?Closed deals in this exact asset class, not just listings.
•
Do you hold a specialty designation?CCIM or SIOR signals deeper technical training.
•
What's the full fee structure?Get it in writing before you sign a rep agreement.
